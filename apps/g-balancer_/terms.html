3. Description of Services

G-Balancer is an offline, browser/device-based tool for formulating and balancing gelato, ice cream, sorbet, and related frozen desserts. Its functions include:

  • an ingredient database with compositional data (sugars, fats, milk solids-not-fat, other solids, water, protein, POD/PAC-type sweetening and anti-freezing values);
  • recipe balancing, batch and per-batch scaling, machine capacity and overrun handling, evaporation and pasteurisation/process calculations, storage-cabinet and shelf-life settings;
  • stabiliser, emulsifier and protein (S&E) suggestions and auto-formulation helpers;
  • a Quick Check panel and metrics dashboard reporting indicative values such as Sweetness (relative sweetening power), Scoopability / hardness at serving temperature, Brix, freezing-point depression, water and solids balance, mouthfeel/texture indicators, Relative Volume Index, and estimated Glycemic Index (GI) and Glycemic Load (GL) per serving;
  • multilingual interface, light/system/dark themes, and export of recipes, labels and data (PDF, Excel/CSV, JSON) to your own device.

Free demo and full version. The App can be used free of charge in a limited demo mode (a read-only Classic Gelato preset). A one-time in-app purchase (the "Full Version") unlocks the complete ingredient database, all presets, creating, editing, saving and loading your own recipes and ingredients, and the export features (PDF, Excel, CSV, JSON, labels).
