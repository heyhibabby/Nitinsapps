GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked

Does G-Balancer need an internet connection or an account?

No. The app runs entirely on your device, with no sign-up and no cloud sync. Your recipes stay in local storage, and exports are your backup.

Can I use the nutrition panel on a commercial label?

Panels are formatted to the layouts required in several countries and are a strong starting point, but you remain responsible for verifying values and wording against the regulations that apply where you sell.

Are the GI and GL values medical advice?

No. Glycemic index and load are indicative estimates for recipe development only and must not be used for diabetes management or "low-GI" marketing claims.

Can I move recipes between devices?

Yes — export your recipes to a file and import them on another device, and print labels and reports at any time.
