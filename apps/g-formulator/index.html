GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked

Does G-Formulator need an internet connection or an account?

No. The app runs completely on your device, with no registration and no cloud dependency. Your formulas remain securely in local storage.

Can I use the nutrition panel on a commercial label?

Panels are formatted to standard international layouts, providing a robust draft for labeling, though final verification against local packaging regulations is advised.

Can I scale formulas for commercial production batches?

Yes. The batch scaling module allows you to scale ingredients by total weight, volume, or component percentages with automatic stabilizer recalculation.

Can I move formulas between devices?

Yes — export your formulas to JSON, Excel/CSV, or PDF at any time and import them seamlessly onto another device.
