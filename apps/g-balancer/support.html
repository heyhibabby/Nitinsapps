1. Technical & Formula Support

If you have any questions regarding batch scaling, evaporation modeling, Brix/PAC calculations, stabilizer dosing factors, or machine setup, our engineering team is available via email.

When contacting support, please include your device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), and the specific ingredient or calculation profile you are testing.

2. Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?
No. G-Balancer is designed to operate completely offline in your kitchen or production facility. There are no cloud dependencies or mandatory login screens.

How do I backup or transfer my formulations?
Navigate to Menu → Export in the app to download your recipes as a PDF report, an Excel/CSV spreadsheet, or a complete JSON archive to your device storage.

3. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play & Apple Compliance)

In full compliance with Google Play Data Safety and Apple App Store Review Guidelines, users have complete autonomy over their personal data.

Because G-Balancer uses a local-first computing model without accounts or cloud storage:

  • No Cloud Accounts: We do not create, host, or store any remote user profiles or server-side accounts.
  • Instant Local Data Deletion: All your recipes, custom ingredients, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device. You can permanently erase all stored data at any time by clearing the app data/cache in your device settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Email Correspondence Purge: If you have previously communicated with our support desk via email and wish to have all past correspondence permanently deleted from our support inbox, simply send an email to G-Balancer@outlook.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - G-Balancer. All records will be expunged within 14 business days.
