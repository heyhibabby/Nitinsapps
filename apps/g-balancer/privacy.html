Privacy Policy

Gelato & Ice Cream Balancer (G-Balancer)
Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Privacy version: 2026.10.12
App Store search name: Gelato & Ice Cream Balancer · Home-screen name: G-Balancer · Subtitle: Precise Visual Recipe Balancer

1. Summary (plain English)

  • The App has no accounts, no login, no cloud sync and no servers of ours that store your data.
  • Your recipes, custom ingredients and settings are stored only on your own device. We never receive them.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash telemetry.
  • The only data processed outside your device relates to the one-time purchase: Apple/Google process the payment, and our purchase-management provider RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID and receipt data so the App knows whether the Full Version is unlocked. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.
  • The App occasionally checks a small static file to see whether our Terms or this Policy have a newer version. No personal data is sent.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or receive: your name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment card details, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, advertising IDs, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or your recipes and ingredient data. The App contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no social-media trackers.

4. Purchase Data (RevenueCat, Apple, Google)

The App offers a free demo and a one-time in-app purchase (the Full Version). To process and verify the purchase:

WhoWhat they processWhy
Apple (App Store) / Google (Google Play) Your payment and account details, under their own privacy policies. We never see these. Take payment and issue the purchase
RevenueCat, Inc. (our service provider) An anonymous App User ID generated automatically by the SDK; the store receipt / transaction record; the product purchased and whether the Full Version entitlement is active; app version, platform and OS version; and technical data such as IP address and country that accompany every network request. Verify the purchase, unlock the Full Version, support Restore Purchase on a new device

This data is not linked by us to your name or email and is used only to provide the purchase and unlock function. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold. RevenueCat acts as our processor under its own terms and privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy). Data may be processed in the United States and other countries; see Section 11.

The App also keeps a local flag on your device recording whether the Full Version is unlocked, so it can start quickly and work offline.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

To function, the App saves data in your device's local storage (browser localStorage or equivalent app storage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

Stored locally Purpose
Recipes, batches, ingredient selections and notes So your work is still there when you reopen the App
Custom / edited ingredient database entries To keep your own ingredient data
Machine, storage cabinet, overrun, process and serving settings To remember your production setup
Interface preferences (language, light/system/dark theme, units °C/°F) To restore your chosen settings
Purchase-unlocked flag To remember whether the Full Version is unlocked
Terms / Privacy acceptance record (version numbers and date you accepted) To ask for your acceptance again only when these documents change
Recently used values and UI state Convenience

You control this data. You can delete it at any time using the App's reset/clear functions, or by clearing app data or uninstalling the App. Deletion is permanent and irreversible: we hold no copy and cannot restore it. Your purchase is not lost by this; it can be recovered with Restore Purchase.

6. Exports and Sharing

The App can generate PDF, Excel/CSV, JSON and label exports of your recipes, costings and ingredient database. These files are produced entirely on your device. In the mobile app they are handed to your operating system's share/save sheet. If you then email, print, upload or share them, that transfer is under your control and governed by the policies of whichever service you use.

7. Other Network Requests

The App is designed to work offline for calculation and recipe work. When a connection is available it may make these requests:

  • RevenueCat / App Store / Google Play for purchases and Restore (Section 4);
  • A static "version" file on our website, which tells the App the current version numbers of the Terms and this Policy. This is an ordinary web request: our host may log technical data such as IP address and time as normal web-server operation, but nothing about you, your recipes or your purchase is sent;
  • Web fonts (e.g. Google Fonts) and document-generation libraries (e.g. jsPDF, SheetJS) from public content delivery networks, used for typography and to build your PDF/Excel exports.

When such a resource is requested, the provider's servers may automatically log technical data (such as IP address, browser type and time of request). None of your recipe data is sent to these providers. The App makes no other network calls: no telemetry, no analytics, no user-data uploads. Links to the Terms and this Policy open in your browser or an in-app browser and are governed by the website's own practices.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App does not use cookies for advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking. It uses only local storage strictly necessary for the App to remember your data, preferences, purchase status and acceptance record, as described in Section 5.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. In-app purchases are made through the account holder's Apple or Google account and are subject to that store's parental controls. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights and Retention

Your recipe data is on your device, so you exercise control directly. For purchase data held by RevenueCat and for any email you send us, depending on where you live you may have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority), the California CCPA/CPRA (we do not sell or share personal information), or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To make a request, email G-Balancer@outlook.com; we will act on it with RevenueCat where needed. Note that we cannot identify your purchase record without the anonymous App User ID or your store transaction details, and that deleting purchase data may stop Restore Purchase from working.

Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the purchase service and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them. Locally stored data is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, disk encryption, app sandboxing); we recommend you keep your device secured and take your own exports as backups.

Legal basis (EU/UK): performance of the purchase contract and our legitimate interest in verifying purchases and preventing fraud.
